RS3 Low HP HiScores




Requirements to be listed:
  • HP is at most level 15, i.e. can be reset by Nastroth;
  • Must have at least 100,000 Combat XP;
  • Hiscores must provide enough skill data to calculate the ranking;
  • Your username must be on the tracked roster. To suggest an addition, open an issue or make a pull request.
Some details:
  • Ranking is always determined by the normalised values;
  • Skills and RuneScore come from the RuneScape Hiscores;
  • Quest Points come from the Adventurer's Log. A blank cell means the data is unavailable;
  • Ironman and Hardcore Ironman badges reflect a daily comparison with the standard Hiscores. A matching record can remain after a player changes modes.

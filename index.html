RS3 Low HP HiScores





Requirements to be listed:
  • HP is at most level 15, i.e. can be reset by Nastroth;
  • Must have at least 100,000 Combat XP;
  • Hiscores or Adventurer's Log must provide enough skill data to calculate the ranking;
  • Username must be tracked. To request an addition, open an issue or make a pull request.
Some details:
  • Ranking is always determined by the normalised values;
  • RuneScore and Quest Points may not be available depending on activity and privacy settings.
